# Student workflow — dashboard

The **dashboard** lists activities you are **enrolled** in, usually with:

- **Title** and **type** (assignment, quiz, exam, …).
- **Due date** (when configured).
- **Status**: not started, in progress, submitted, graded (labels vary).

## What you should do

1. Sort mentally by **due date** and **stakes** (exam before optional challenge).
2. Click through to start or resume work.

## If an activity is missing

- Confirm you used the **correct school account**.
- Ask your instructor whether **enrollment** completed.
