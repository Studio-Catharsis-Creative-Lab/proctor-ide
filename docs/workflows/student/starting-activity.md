# Student workflow — starting an activity

## Before you begin

1. **Read the transparency / integrity notice** for this activity type if the product shows one.
2. Close unnecessary applications if the notice says the exam is **monitored** more strictly.
3. Ensure a **stable network**; reconnects may be logged as events in some modes.

## Start

- Click **Start** (or equivalent). The platform records **start time** and may set status to **in progress**.

## During high-stakes sessions

- Follow your instructor’s rules on **resources**, **collaboration**, and **second devices**.
- If you experience a **technical failure**, document the time and contact your instructor with a **screenshot** of any error (no personal data of other students).
