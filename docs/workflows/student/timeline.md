# Student workflow — timeline and version history

## Purpose

The **timeline** helps you:

- Recover from accidental large deletes.
- Understand how your solution evolved (useful when explaining work to a TA).

## How it works (conceptually)

Each meaningful **save** may create a **snapshot** in the platform’s version store. You can **browse** prior versions and **restore** when permitted by activity rules.

## Exams

- Restoration may be **limited** or **disabled** during or after the exam window—follow on-screen messages.
