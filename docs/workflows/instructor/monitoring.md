# Instructor workflow — monitoring and review

## What “monitoring” means here

Monitoring is **technical and behavioral telemetry** configured per activity—**not** a substitute for sound assessment design or human judgment.

## Signals you may have access to

Depending on activity type and deployment:

- **Submission status** (not started / in progress / submitted).
- **Version timeline** (history of saves/commits in the workspace).
- **Code execution logs** (runs initiated from the IDE).
- **Integrity-oriented events** (e.g. focus changes, paste-related markers when enabled)—see [Tracking](../../transparency/tracking-by-activity-type.md).

## Recommended review order

1. Confirm **identity** and **enrollment**.
2. Read **final submission** and timeline for major anomalies.
3. Examine **run logs** for impossible shortcuts vs the stated problem (interpret cautiously).
4. Use **institutional** academic integrity process if you escalate.

## Privacy and minimization

- Access **only** what your role requires for the course.
- Do not share raw logs publicly or with unauthorized peers.
