# Instructor workflow — enrollment and invitations

## Goals

- Ensure **only intended students** can access an activity.
- Maintain an **audit-friendly** trail of who was added and when.

## Methods (as implemented in your deployment)

1. **Invitation codes or links** — single-use or expiring; instructors generate and distribute securely.
2. **Bulk enrollment** — paste or upload student identifiers your admin supports (often institutional UIDs mapped to accounts).

## Procedures

### Issue invitations

- Generate invites with correct **role** (student vs TA).
- Set **expiry** aligned to add/drop deadlines.
- **Revoke** unused invites after the deadline.

### Verify roster

- Spot-check that enrolled students match the **official roster** from your LMS or registrar export.

### Drops and misconduct holds

- Follow institutional rules for **removing access** vs **preserving work product** for appeals.
