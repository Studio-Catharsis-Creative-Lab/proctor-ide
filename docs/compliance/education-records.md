# Education records (FERPA-oriented framing)

!!! note "Jurisdiction"
    This page uses **United States** higher-ed concepts (**FERPA**) as a **familiar anchor**. Institutions outside the US should map to **GDPR**, **PIPEDA**, **local education privacy laws**, etc.

## Likely “education records” in scope

When ProctorIDE stores **personally identifiable** student work, grades, or integrity artifacts tied to an enrolled learner, your registrar / privacy office may classify those as **education records** under **FERPA**—subject to **school official exceptions**, **directory information** rules, and **consent** requirements.

## School official / contractor

If a vendor processes records **on behalf** of the institution for **legitimate educational interests**, institutions typically need:

- A **written agreement** with **FERPA** commitments.
- **Direct control** over **maintenance** and **redisclosure** limits.

## Directory information

**Email addresses** may or may not be directory information—**do not assume**.
