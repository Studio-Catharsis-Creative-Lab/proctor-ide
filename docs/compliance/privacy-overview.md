# Privacy overview

## Roles

- **Students** submit coursework and may generate **telemetry** in integrity modes.
- **Instructors / TAs** access learner data **for educational purposes** tied to their courses.
- **Administrators** operate infrastructure and must follow **least privilege**.

## Processing purposes

- **Deliver instruction**: hosting files, running code, grading.
- **Integrity**: deter and investigate misconduct where permitted by policy.
- **Operations**: reliability, security monitoring, support.

## Transparency

Link students to [Data collected](../transparency/data-collected.md) and [Tracking by activity type](../transparency/tracking-by-activity-type.md) from your syllabus.

## International transfers

If users are in multiple regions, document **transfer mechanisms** (SCCs, adequacy) with your vendors—especially **Firebase / Google** and your **cloud region**.
