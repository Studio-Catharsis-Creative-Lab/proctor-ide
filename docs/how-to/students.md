# How-to — students

## Sign in

Use the **identity method** your instructor specifies (often school Google / Microsoft). If sign-in fails repeatedly, see [Troubleshooting](../workflows/student/troubleshooting.md).

## Find your work

Open the **dashboard**, locate the activity, then **Start** or **Continue**.

## Work in the IDE

- Edit files in the browser IDE; **save** often.
- Use **Run** to execute code in the course sandbox; read **stderr** when builds fail.
- Use **Timeline** to restore earlier versions when allowed ([Timeline](../workflows/student/timeline.md)).

## Understand monitoring

Before high-stakes activities, read [Tracking by activity type](../transparency/tracking-by-activity-type.md) and [Data collected](../transparency/data-collected.md).

## Submit

Use **Submit** when your attempt is final unless your instructor allows multiple submissions.

## Get help

- **Technical**: instructor or campus IT with timestamps and error text (no secrets).
- **Grade disputes**: follow your syllabus’s **regrade** process.
