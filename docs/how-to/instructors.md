# How-to — instructors and TAs

## Create an activity

1. Open the **teacher admin** area (exact UI label may vary by deployment).
2. Choose **activity type** (assignment, challenge, quiz, exam)—this drives **tracking intensity** ([matrix](../reference/activity-types-matrix.md)).
3. Set **title**, **due date**, and **visibility** per course policy.
4. Upload a **starter template** or attach files so students begin from a consistent baseline.

## Enroll students

1. Generate **invitations** (single or bulk) from the enrollment workflow ([Enrollment](../workflows/instructor/enrollment.md)).
2. Share codes through your LMS or syllabus—avoid posting restricted codes in public channels.

## Monitor progress

- Use **submissions** and **activity views** described in [Monitoring & review](../workflows/instructor/monitoring.md).
- **Run logs** and **tracking events** visibility follows role ([Instructor visibility](../transparency/visibility-instructors.md)).

## Grade or review

- Align rubrics with what the platform **automatically** captures vs what requires **human judgment**.
- Document integrity concerns using institutional procedures, not ad-hoc screenshots alone—see [Incident response](../procedures/incident-response.md).

## Collaboration comments

- When enabled, comments attach to **lines of code**; moderate tone per course policy.
