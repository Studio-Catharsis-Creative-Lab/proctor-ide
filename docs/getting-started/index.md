# Getting started

This section covers **first login**, **supported environments**, and **terms** used across ProctorIDE docs.

## Prerequisites

- **Account**: Invitations are issued by your instructor or institution; you sign in with **Firebase Authentication** (e.g. Google) unless your school configures otherwise.
- **Browser**: Desktop Chromium-based or Firefox recommended for the MVP; follow [Environments](environments.md) for details.
- **Network**: Outbound HTTPS to the API; WebSocket access for real-time features (e.g. comments) as allowed by your school.
