# Instructor and TA visibility

Teaching staff use aggregated and **per-student** views to **grade**, **support**, and **investigate integrity concerns**.

## Typical access

- **Roster / enrollment** for activities they manage.
- **Submissions** and **files** for those activities.
- **Execution logs** and **tracking events** where enabled by activity type.
- **Comment threads** on student code.

## Least privilege

- **TAs** receive permissions **your institution configures**—do not assume global admin access.
- **Cross-course** access should be **denied** unless staff are explicitly enrolled or given admin roles.

## Audit posture

Access for grading should be **documented** (who graded whom). High-stakes integrity reviews should follow [Incident response](../procedures/incident-response.md).
