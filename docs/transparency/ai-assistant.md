# AI assistant — transparency and exploration

Some courses integrate **AI coding assistants** (IDE plugins, campus-wide licenses, or embedded chat). This page frames **expectations** when AI touches assessment workflows.

## Institutional stance comes first

Your syllabus should state whether AI assistance is:

- **Allowed** with citation,
- **Limited** (e.g. explanations only, no full solutions),
- **Prohibited** for graded work.

The platform does **not** replace honor-code enforcement—**instructors** define rules.

## Data implications

If students use an **external** assistant:

- **Prompts and code** may leave the institution’s boundary—treat as **third-party processing**.
- Update [Vendors & subprocessors](../procedures/vendors-subprocessors.md) when introducing new AI tools.

If the product embeds an assistant:

- Expect **logging** similar to other editor actions where integrity modes apply.
- Students should receive **clear notice** before the assistant sends context off-device.

## Pedagogy

- Prefer **formative** challenges with AI allowed before **summative** exams with AI restricted.
- Design tasks that reward **process artifacts** (tests, explanations) that are harder to fake.
