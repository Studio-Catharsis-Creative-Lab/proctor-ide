# Transparency hub

ProctorIDE is designed so **students**, **instructors**, and **institutions** can understand **what is recorded**, **why**, and **who can access it**.

## Principles

1. **Disclose before high stakes** — Students should see clear notices **before** starting exams when tracking is strong.
2. **Least privilege** — Access matches [roles](../reference/roles-permissions.md).
3. **Institutional policy wins** — Product defaults do not replace your school’s integrity code or privacy notices.

!!! note
    This documentation describes **typical** product behavior. Your deployment may enable or disable features; confirm with your administrator.
